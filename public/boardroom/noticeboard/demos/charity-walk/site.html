Kia ora,
I'm Kōkiri.

THIS SECTION IS YOURS TO FREELY EDIT

Cancer has touched my whānau very personally. Over the last two years, both my koro and my auntie have faced cancer. I wanted to do something difficult enough to honour them and everyone walking that same road.

I'm walking Te Araroa from Cape Reinga to Bluff — 3,000 kilometres, one day at a time. Along the way, I hope to whakapiki mōhiotanga awareness of how deeply cancer affects Kiwi families, and encourage people to support the Cancer Society.

Every evening I update this website from my phone with where I've been, where I'm heading next, and a little story from the day. I read every message while I update the site, and it's something I genuinely look forward to.

Ngā mihi nui — thank you for walking alongside me.
