Small theatre. Live performance. Bold storytelling.

Clementine Street is an independent 200-seat theatre made for live drama at human scale. The room asks for attention rather than volume, and performances unfold with the audience close enough to feel each shift in tone.

Set inside a converted warehouse at the edge of Wāperiki, the building keeps its timber trusses and blackened steel bones. What remains is deliberate and spare: a theatre that trusts writing, actors and silence.

The Building

Before it was a theatre, this address worked as a harbour warehouse: practical, weathered, and built for weight. The conversion kept that character, then shaped it for performance with careful acoustics and a compact rake that holds the room together.

The auditorium seats around 200 people, with blackened steel, exposed timber and soft light doing most of the visual work. There is no ornamental distance between stage and audience, only clear sightlines and materials that carry sound honestly.

Everything here is tuned for live drama rather than spectacle. Actors can read the room; audiences can read the breath between lines. By design, everyone becomes part of the same event.

Did You Know?

Actors often hear audience reactions as clearly as the audience hears them.

Built to Listen

Clementine Street is a theatre where silence matters. Performances rely on intimacy, and stories are experienced in the room with focus, attention and shared energy.

Hire Clementine Street

The theatre is available for dramatic productions, rehearsals, staged readings, small creative launches, theatre workshops and private performance events.

Technical Overview

Approximately 200 fixed seats on a gentle rake with clear sightlines.
Proscenium stage with configurable masking. Width 8.5m, depth 6m, height to grid 5.2m.
Lighting grid with digital control - ETC Ion, LED wash + profile stock.
Stereo front-of-house sound, 4-way monitor, playback via QLab.
Backstage: 2 dressing rooms (4 persons), green room, accessible WC.
Loading access by arrangement via roller door on Clementine Lane.

Accessibility

  • Step-free entrance via side lane - level threshold, no steps
  • 4 accessible seating spaces in Row D and Row K with companion seating
  • Accessible toilet on ground floor
  • Hearing assistance available - loop and personal receivers by request
  • Staff assistance by request - contact us before your visit
  • Guide dogs welcome - water bowl available

If you have specific access requirements, email clementinesttheatre@rkdb.nz or call (07) 555 0148 and we will hold suitable seats.
