The Glass Room

By Mara Vale · Directed by Elias North

When three adult siblings return to clear their late father’s house, a locked room forces them to confront a version of their childhood none of them remembers in quite the same way.

Every family keeps something behind the door.

A taut new drama about memory, inheritance and the stories people protect long after they stop believing them. Built for Clementine’s intimate space — direct, immediate, and alive in the room.

Director’s Note

Elias North

“The Glass Room is about the stories families build together, and what happens when one person finally refuses to keep telling them.

We rehearsed it in the theatre itself, with the heating off. You could hear the building settle. That’s what the play wants — a room that has been holding its breath.”
