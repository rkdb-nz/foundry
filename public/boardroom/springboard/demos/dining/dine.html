Meet Clementine

I started cooking because I liked feeding my friends. Not impressing them - feeding them. The best nights were always around a table that was a little too small, with a little too much food, and nowhere to be afterwards. Clementines is that table. We cook what we want to eat, we pour what we want to drink, and we try to make you feel like you belong here. Nothing pretentious. Just excellent ingredients, treated simply, with a lot of care. Come hungry. Stay late.

- Clementine
